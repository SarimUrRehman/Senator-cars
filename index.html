Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Our mobile detailing unit carries onboard spot-free water and quiet power so we can operate completely self-contained at most homes and workplaces. If an outdoor tap or power outlet happens to be available nearby, we're happy to plug in for multi-stage polishing jobs.
An Essential detail typically takes 1.5 to 2 hours. Our Premium Full Detail runs around 3.5 to 4 hours, while Ultimate packages, paint correction, or ceramic coatings take 5 to 6+ hours depending on vehicle size and paint condition.
We service Auckland & surrounding areas—including Central Auckland, North Shore, East Auckland, West Auckland, and South Auckland—with zero call-out fee within our standard metro zone.
If you have a garage or covered carport, we can complete almost every service rain or shine. For open driveways during heavy rain, we'll contact you ahead of time to reschedule to the next clear slot at no charge.
Starting prices cover standard hatchbacks and sedans. Large 7-seater SUVs, utes, or vehicles with heavy pet hair or mud carry a modest surcharge that we confirm with you clearly before any work begins.
